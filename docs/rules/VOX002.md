# VOX002: Excluded-scope headings

## What it checks

A heading framed around non-goals or excluded scope in a long-lived page.

## Basis

Heuristic. A heading framed around non-goals or excluded scope suggests a planning boundary rather than a capability readers came to understand.

## Why it matters

A durable heading names the capability or boundary the reader came to understand. Plans and ADRs can record excluded project scope.

## Example

```markdown
---
kind: howto
---
## What is not included
```

## Instead

```markdown
---
kind: howto
---
## Supported workflows
```

## Exceptions

Plans and ADRs can record non-goals. Some contractual boundaries belong in a reference; suppress such a heading with a reason when positive wording would obscure the boundary.

## Inputs

Only visible heading text and link labels are read. Body prose, inline code, and URLs cannot trigger a heading phrase.
