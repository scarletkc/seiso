# EVD001: Evaluative claims without evidence

## What it checks

A comparative quality claim without a nearby source or measurement in a declared document.

## Basis

Heuristic. A comparative phrase with no nearby source or measurement suggests an assessment readers cannot verify.

## Why it matters

A claim of relative quality needs a source or observable result near enough for readers to assess it.

## Example

```markdown
---
kind: howto
---
This implementation is faster.
```

## Instead

```markdown
---
kind: howto
---
This implementation is faster in the [benchmark](https://example.org/benchmark).
```

## Exceptions

A link, measurement (including a table), or explicit benchmark context in the same or one adjacent content block supplies evidence. A recommendation may also be justified in its own paragraph or demonstrated by a following actionable block. Comparative performance claims still require measurement or source evidence. The window stays in the same section and container. Conditional instructions, quoted phrases, and explicit sample-output contexts are excluded. A recommendation without a comparative claim or recognizable support remains undetermined, preserving its suppression. Evidence presence does not establish evidence quality.

## Inputs

Visible prose supplies the claim. Links and measured values supply evidence. Heading claims are excluded. No external link is fetched.
