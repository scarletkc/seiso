# MIX001: Conflicting section responsibilities

## What it checks

A section with an explicit project-decision heading or decision prose and at least two direct prose paragraphs in a how-to, reference, or runbook.

## Basis

A document kind defines its responsibility. Extended design decisions belong in a decision record linked from the relevant procedure or contract.

## Example

```markdown
---
kind: howto
---
## Design decisions

We chose a shared queue to coordinate workers.

The alternative required each worker to maintain its own state.
```

## Instead

```markdown
---
kind: howto
---
## Connect to the queue

Run `queue connect`.

See the decision record for the queue selection rationale.
```

## Exceptions

This conservative initial rule reports extended rationale only. It does not infer every possible responsibility conflict. A short ADR pointer and an orientation section explaining a feature's benefits are allowed. Opaque containers preserve incomplete suppression state. Parent sections do not inherit child paragraphs or roles.

## Inputs

Section roles and direct paragraph structure are read. Document kinds are resolved by the normal frontmatter and path policy.
