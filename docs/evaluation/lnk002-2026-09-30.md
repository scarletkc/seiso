# LNK002 fresh holdout

Recorded on 2026-09-30 for [issue 11](https://github.com/scarletkc/seiso/issues/11).
LNK002 stays in preview. Its fresh holdout found 451 diagnoses: 96 true
positives, 306 false positives, and 49 uncertain, a conservative precision of
21.3% against the 95% that the [promotion policy](policy.md#stable-promotion)
requires. Most false positives come from site generators whose anchor rules
LNK002 does not model.

## Inputs

The implementation was frozen at `136585a72b7317df0a9893348350d04f70b8de91`,
which is `main` at `7f8371f` plus the
[heading name decision](lnk002-heading-names-2026-09-30.md) and the cohort
tooling. The [selection specification](../../corpus/results/lnk002/fresh-v1/sources.json)
was committed before any source was pinned. It fixes 25 repositories in four
batches, each with a documentation path scope and the root `README.md`.

Batch 1 alone reached the 100-diagnosis threshold, so batches 2 to 4 were never
pinned or read. Its ten sources hold 2,230 Markdown documents, fixed with their
original hashes in the [corpus lock](../../corpus/results/lnk002/fresh-v1/batch-1/corpus.lock.json).
The [site profile](../../corpus/results/lnk002/fresh-v1/batch-1/sites.json)
maps documents to the sites that publish them, from each source's pinned
generator configuration or navigation. Every document has kind `unknown`,
because LNK002 applies to every kind. The [run](../../corpus/results/lnk002/fresh-v1/batch-1/run/run.json)
produced byte-identical output with sources, documents, and tree entries
reversed.

## Labels

A diagnosis was judged on the page its readers open: the site generator's
output when a reviewed site entry covers the target, and GitHub's repository
view otherwise. Two agents labeled all 451 independently, and each sealed its
labels before seeing the other's. They agreed on 443 (98.2%). After checking
the renderer, seven of the eight disagreements resolved as false positives and
one as uncertain. The [bound labels](../../corpus/results/lnk002/fresh-v1/batch-1/annotations/labels-links.json)
keep both original reviews and the reason for each resolution. No person
labeled.

## Decision

The sample and dual-label requirements are met and the precision requirement
fails, so the gate fails. Vitest supplied 268 diagnoses (59.4%). A source
above half of the sample needs the owner's acceptance, which was not given
because precision already fails. No rule code, rule page, or preview status
changed after the freeze. Promotion needs another fresh holdout after the
fixes, planned in [issue 58](https://github.com/scarletkc/seiso/issues/58)
with a per-source cap in place of the owner's judgment.
